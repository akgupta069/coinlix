Overview

CoinLix ("the app", "we", "us") is a casual games and rewards app published by an independent developer for the Indian market. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the CoinLix Android app and this page.

What we collect

CoinLix collects the minimum needed to keep your coin balance safe across devices and to run the referral program:

DataWhenWhy
Name, email, profile photoOnly if you choose "Sign in with Google"Create your account, show your profile, sync coins to the cloud
Coin balance & transaction historyAs you play, check in, or redeemTrack earnings and redemptions accurately
Referral code & referral relationshipsWhen you share or enter a codeCredit the right accounts for the two-tier referral program
Daily check-in streakAs you check inCalculate your current streak and reward tier

We do not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or files.

How we use it

  • To operate core features — coin balance, wallet, games, referrals, and daily check-in.
  • To keep your progress in sync if you use CoinLix on more than one device.
  • To detect obvious abuse of the referral or reward system (for example, self-referrals).

We do not use your data for purposes unrelated to running the app, and we do not build advertising profiles from it.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. It is shared only with the infrastructure providers that run CoinLix:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore database) — hosts your account and app data. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the security of the app and its users.

Guest mode

You can use CoinLix without signing in. In guest mode, your coins and activity are stored only on your device (never uploaded to our servers) and are lost if you uninstall the app or clear its data. Signing in with Google later carries your guest progress into your synced account.

Security

Your account data is protected by Firebase security rules that restrict every user's data to that user alone — no other account can read or write it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we apply industry-standard practices throughout.

Retention & deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and associated data, email us at the address below — we'll action verified requests within 30 days.

Children

CoinLix is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we'll remove it.

Ads & monetization

CoinLix does not currently show third-party ads — in-app "watch an ad to claim" moments are placeholders while the reward economy is tuned. If real ad partners are introduced, this policy will be updated first, and ad networks will only receive the minimum data required for ad delivery under their own privacy terms.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will update the "last updated" date above; continued use of CoinLix after a change means you accept the revised policy.

Delete Your Account & Data

How to permanently delete your CoinLix account, coin balance, and personal data.

How to request deletion

CoinLix does not yet have an in-app self-service delete button, so deletion requests are handled by email:

  • Send an email to kushal142529@gmail.com from the same email address you used to sign in with Google.
  • Use the subject line "Delete my CoinLix account" and mention the email/Google account linked to the app.
  • We verify the request and permanently delete your account within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your Firebase Authentication account (name, email, profile photo).
  • Your coin balance and full transaction history.
  • Your referral code, referral relationships, and referral earnings records.
  • Your daily check-in streak and profile data.

This deletion is permanent and cannot be undone — any unredeemed coin balance is also removed.

Guest mode accounts

If you used CoinLix without signing in, your data was only ever stored on your device and never uploaded to our servers. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage from your phone's Settings deletes it immediately — no email request needed.
